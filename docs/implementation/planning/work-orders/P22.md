## Deliverables

Unify jobs around immutable envelopes, search tokens, stale-response rejection, progress and
cancellation. Yield within expensive candidate/trial work, keep computation off the UI thread, add
bounded caches/lazy ruleset/cohort loading/checkpoints, and cap parallelism/memory. Partial results
and resume points cannot contaminate completed-result caches.

## Acceptance

Stale results cannot overwrite newer state. Long single-candidate work remains responsive. Cache
identity includes ruleset/scenario/policy/cohort/random inputs, and resumed jobs equal uninterrupted
jobs under the same manifest.
