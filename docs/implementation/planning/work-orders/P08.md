## Deliverables

Implement windup/launch/recovery, attack speed, resets, casts/channels, projectile/tick delivery and
hit/miss resolution. Compile serializable scripted and conditional policies with target/readiness
rules and wait/skip/fail behavior. Unavailable actions may attack, move, wait or choose an
alternative only when policy permits. Item actives and summoners use the same action system.

## Acceptance

The W-to-AA chronology regression passes through the real executor. Repeated actions obey resource,
cooldown, form, range and control state; interrupted or moving actions alter later events. Compared
builds use the same policy unless policy search is explicitly requested.
