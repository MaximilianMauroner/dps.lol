Implement coordinates, hitboxes, movement, dashes/blinks, displacement, terrain, area/line/cone
targeting, vision and target validity. Represent policies for enemy movement and aiming separately

reproducible. Area effects respect eligibility. Movement cannot create free attacks or stacks when
movement is forbidden.
