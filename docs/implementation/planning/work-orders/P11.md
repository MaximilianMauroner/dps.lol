## Deliverables

Separate exact analytical evaluation, labeled mean-state approximation, seeded trajectory and
Monte Carlo modes with versioned RNG law/draw streams. Define burst, fixed-window, sustained,
kill-coverage and first-death/final-elimination TTK objectives, horizons, warm-up, censoring and
ties. Add paired trials, uncertainty and independent finalist validation.

## Acceptance

Degenerate crit and finite exact cases match deterministic/enumerated results. Nonlinear crit,
cooldown, health and proc cases demonstrate why average-state damage is not exact TTK/probability.
Replay preserves seed configuration and mode labels.
