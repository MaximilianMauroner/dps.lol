## Deliverables

Generate source/type/action/prevention/heal/survival deltas and first-divergence explanations from
lineage. Add paired controlled ablations, labeled non-additivity and engine-evaluated sensitivity
sweeps across health/resistance/time/position/behavior. Expose casts, blocked actions, resources,
stacks, cooldowns, shields, deaths and random draws.

## Acceptance

Explanations reconcile to frozen inputs/events. Interaction examples identify the actual additional
proc/action/survival interval. Nonmonotonic rankings and multiple breakpoints remain visible, and
synthetic sensitivity is never presented as observed win-rate evidence.
