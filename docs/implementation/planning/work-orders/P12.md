## Deliverables

Define item instances, slots, locks, ownership, recipes, upgrades, sales, consumables, trinkets,
boots, unique groups, champion/mode restrictions and purchase accounting. Distinguish total final
inventory budget from additional gold and preserve meaningful acquisition state.

## Acceptance

Legal-state validation covers components and duplicate rules without a universal shortcut. Locked
items, upgrades, sales, remaining gold and executable transactions round-trip and reject illegal
states. Observed inventory history never grants free stacks or credits.
