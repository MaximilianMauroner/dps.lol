## Deliverables

Implement incoming damage, shields, healing, regeneration, lifesteal/omnivamp, prevention, spell
blocks, invulnerability, CC, tenacity, cleanse and immunity. Model death, ownership, executes,
post-death effects, summons, resurrection and respawn. Report first death, terminal elimination and
alive/dead/reviving at the horizon separately.

## Acceptance

Defenses and survival can change damage and TTK. Shield/health/prevention/overheal reconcile without
double counting. Projectile-after-death, simultaneous deaths, execute/revive boundaries and
post-death entities are covered.
